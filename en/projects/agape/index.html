Bringing AI into Agapé

An audit of AI use across 9 departments and an LLM-based online reputation monitoring tool put into service.

Role
AI & process integration officer
Period
April to August 2026
Technologies
React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, API Anthropic, Tavily

Context

Agapé's management wanted to know where AI could really help its teams, and to try out concrete tools.

What was built

  • Audit of 9 departments against a 16-criteria grid, with costed recommendations handed to general management (estimated return of 6.5 times the cost for the main recommendation).
  • Online reputation monitoring tool in React and TypeScript: the Tavily API gathers more than 30 sources per run, then a Claude model (Anthropic API) sorts them. Put into service during the assignment.
  • Feasibility study and prototype of a Node.js middleware connecting Shopify, the ERP and Brevo, across 3 synchronisation flows.
  • Python script for batch anonymisation of supplier PDF sheets.
  • Handover note covering all 4 projects at the end of the assignment.

Outcome

One tool in service, costed recommendations handed to management and a documented handover.

Limits and next steps

The middleware stayed at prototype stage: it was not deployed to production.
